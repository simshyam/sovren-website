Profile

Bhupinder is the founder of United Accounting, a tax and bookkeeping firm that has served the GTA for over 20 years. His reputation was built brick by brick on client trust: the firm has never run an ad, and its client base has grown entirely through relationships and referrals. But the processes behind that reputation were old-fashioned and small-scale, and they stood in the way of his real goal: retiring in the foreseeable future and passing the torch to a new director.

This engagement focused on the Transformation and Regulation function of the Sovren Framework. When Sovren came in, the client management was split across paper, local Excel files, and email. Nothing was connected, nothing updated automatically, and internal workflows ran on static spreadsheets. The tech stack was undocumented; tools had been added over the years with no map of what each one did, who owned it, or what was redundant. Before the firm could change hands, it needed systems a new director could actually inherit.

Project Outcomes

While Bhupinder was also interested in Online Visibility, Core Operations took precedence: a firm cannot be handed over on paper and memory.

  • Full TaxDome implementation with custom workflow pipelines, templates, and client management systems
  • Customized client portal configured through TaxDome to streamline client communication, document requests, e-signatures, and billing
  • Custom onboarding materials to move clients smoothly onto the new portal
  • Every system documented with SOPs, linked to a dynamic Claude project Help Desk for troubleshooting ongoing issues
  • Structured transition plan to retire the manual workflow trackers and move fully onto TaxDome
